about

born in 2001

since 2023 student in the Class New Genres / Tanja Widmann
Academy of Fine Arts Munich

gina-marie.bross@gmx.de

My artistic practice examines the experiences of the subject in urban, public, and social space. Working with diverse materials and formats, I investigate how spatial politics shape perception, positioning, and the subject's agency. Through interaction with and intervention in existing spatial structures, these dynamics are exposed and made perceptible to the viewer.
